# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place for students, teachers, parents and developers.

**Be respectful.** Assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and keep discussion constructive.

**Be inclusive.** Use plain language, and do not discriminate on the basis of identity, background, language or ability. Hindi and English are both welcome.

**Be safe.** No harassment, threats, personal attacks, spam, or sharing of other people's personal data (including children's data).

**Be honest.** Do not submit fabricated results, misleading claims, or code you do not have the right to share.

### Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. Report concerns through a GitHub issue marked *conduct* or through the private reporting in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This summary is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
